--- interpretation ---
The tree above reproduces app-analytics.html's real structure through the
whole chain (amazing_a11y::AxTree -> a11y_bridge::build_element -> GPUI div
tree -> AccessKit -> accesskit_unix -> AT-SPI2 D-Bus -> pyatspi client):
  - the page's nav landmark region with its Home/Section/Search links and
    Menu button
  - the Analytics heading (role 'heading')
  - the data table, its header row (Name/Value/Status column headers) and
    all 8 data rows/cells, with row and cell text matching the fixture
  - GenericContainer nodes (the two nested 'document frame' entries here)
    were correctly rendered as structurally-transparent wrappers rather than
    producing spurious or panicking AccessKit nodes, per the
    debug_assert!("GenericContainer is filtered out...") guard in GPUI's
    div().role() implementation.
Every accessible name visible above (Analytics, Home, Section, Search, Menu,
Name, Value, Status, row N / count / ok) is present in the source fixture, so
this is a faithful, unmodified pass-through of the fixture's real content
through the live AT-SPI2 bus, not a synthetic/hand-built tree.
